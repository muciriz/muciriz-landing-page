Steps to Request Account Deletion

Users can initiate an account and data deletion request through any of the following channels:

Option 1: Web Request (No App Installation Required)

  1. Submit the online form on this page with your registered mobile phone number.
  2. Our customer support team verifies your request via OTP or verification call within 24–48 hours.
  3. Upon verification, your profile, addresses, and app session tokens are scheduled for permanent erasure.

Option 2: From Inside the Muciriz Mobile App

  1. Open the Muciriz mobile app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Go to Account / Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Account Settings > Request Account Deletion.
  4. Confirm using the OTP sent to your registered mobile phone number.

Option 3: Direct Email or Helpline

  1. Send an email to customercare@muciriz.in with subject "Account Deletion Request - [Your Phone Number]".
  2. Or contact our customer helpline at +91 94477 28387 (Mon–Fri, 10 AM – 5 PM IST).

Data Deleted vs. Data Retained

Data Permanently Deleted

  • Personal Profile: Full name, phone number, secondary contacts.
  • Saved Delivery Locations: House/flat address, landmarks, GPS coordinates.
  • Authentication Credentials: Active session tokens and login credentials.
  • Device Tokens: Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Shopping Data: Active cart items, product notes, and branch preferences.

Data Retained & Statutory Rationale

  • Invoices & Financial Records: Order bills, tax amounts, and GST receipts.
  • Statutory Requirement: Mandated under Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 & Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for commercial tax audit records.
  • Retention Period: Preserved in an encrypted, access-restricted offline archive for the statutory duration (7–8 financial years), after which they are permanently shredded. All links to your active phone number are severed.

Retention Periods & Erasure Timelines

  • Request Verification: 24 to 48 hours to confirm identity and prevent unauthorized deletion.
  • Profile & Address Erasure: Completed within 7 to 30 calendar days across active production databases.
  • Encrypted Backup Snapshots: Automatically overwritten within 60 days in accordance with routine backup rotation.
  • Tax & Statutory Invoices: Retained for the statutory 7–8 financial years as mandated by Indian tax laws.
